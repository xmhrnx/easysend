Three ways to send

Next door or across the world.

EasySend picks the shortest path. Nearby devices talk directly; everything else gets a code or a link.

Nearby

Devices on the same network find each other on their own. Pick one, drop your files — they go straight across, encrypted, at full Wi‑Fi speed.

Works with LocalSend

Internet code

Far away? Share a short code. The other side types it or scans it, and the transfer is end‑to‑end encrypted with a key only the two of you hold.

Speaks open protocols

Nearby transfers use the LocalSend protocol and internet transfers use Magic Wormhole, so EasySend works with apps you or your friends already have.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license. Read it, build it, or help make it better.

Private by design

What you send is nobody else’s business.

Nearby transfers go straight from one device to the other. Internet transfers are end‑to‑end encrypted. Nothing you send ever reaches the developer.
